# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Software engineers working in local repositories on Windows, macOS, and Linux. They need to understand code, make reviewed changes, run commands and tests, and iterate without leaving the desktop workspace.

## Product Purpose

Neru is a local-first AI coding agent. It brings project exploration, search, editing, Git, terminal work, model chat, approvals, diffs, and checkpoints into one focused engineering workflow: think, understand, build, run, observe, and refine.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and crafted. The experience should feel warm, capable, trustworthy, and human while staying fast and focused. The name 練る expresses refinement through repeated thought and work.

## Anti-references

Avoid cyberpunk, gamer, neon, generic SaaS, glass, anime, and overloaded interfaces. Borrow clear interaction patterns from capable coding tools without copying their identity. Avoid decorative gradients, sparkles, and robot imagery in the product UI.

## Design Principles

1. Show the next useful engineering action clearly.
2. Make proposed changes and commands reviewable before execution.
3. Keep the workspace quiet enough for long coding sessions.
4. Preserve speed and clarity as projects and conversations grow.
5. Make the mascot a small expression of Neru's character without competing with the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable type, sufficient contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic controls, and reduced-motion support. Keep light and dark themes usable. The mascot's optional reactions must not block the coding workflow.
